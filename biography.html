About Testy Money ITG

Inspirational. Talented. Gifted.

Testimony Ayomide Ajayi, professionally known as Testy Money ITG, is a Nigerian rapper, singer and songwriter from Igbara-Oke, Ondo State.

Known for blending hip-hop and Afrobeats with real-life storytelling, Testy Money ITG is building his identity through original music, collaborations, performances and a growing digital audience.

His notable releases include JAGABAN, The Light (Imole) and GAZA featuring Portable, released in 2026. His music reflects ambition, personal experiences and a desire to create lasting impact.

ITG stands for Inspirational, Talented and Gifted — the creative identity behind the artist and the community that supports his journey.

His supporters are known as ITGs. The name represents the people who connect with the ITG message, stream the music, share the work and grow with the artist.
