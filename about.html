About the artist

Testy Money ITG

Testimony Ayomide Ajayi, professionally known as Testy Money ITG (born May 17), is a Nigerian rapper, singer and songwriter from Igbara-Oke, Ondo State.

His music blends hip-hop and Afrobeats with real-life storytelling, personal experiences and an ambition to create lasting impact. His debut song “JAGABAN” helped build recognition around his local community and schools.

ITG means Inspirational, Talented and Gifted — the identity behind his artistic direction.
