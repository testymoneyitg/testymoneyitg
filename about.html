About the artist

Testy Money ITG

Professionally known as Testy Money ITG, Testimony Ayomide Ajayi is a Nigerian rapper, singer and songwriter from Igbara-Oke, Ondo State.

Biography

Testy Money ITG is an emerging Nigerian artist whose sound brings together hip-hop, rap and Afrobeats with real-life storytelling. His artistic identity is built around ITG — Inspirational, Talented and Gifted — and a desire to grow, create impact and build a lasting musical career.

His releases include GAZA featuring Portable, JAGABAN and the Light of the World project, including The Light (Imole).
